# Globe data update

The continent renderer is adapted from the supplied globe-main repository as described in source comments. The map now loads the supplied `locations.tsv` directly, with 12,773 validated rows. Its `lat`, `lon`, and `size` columns define coordinates and logarithmic marker area. All location points use muted teal (`#427a68`), matching the page accents; no biome column is required. Every current size is 1. No synthetic location data is used. Each location is filled separately in stable row order, with continuous horizon opacity. This prevents overlapping points from blinking when they cross depth buckets: compound paths previously merged their opacity within a bucket but accumulated it across buckets. Land cells now form a connected geographic honeycomb, classified from the supplied land samples. Adjacent cells share spherical vertices (including the longitude seam), and cells are clipped at the horizon. This removes the spacing between the former independently placed hexagons. Cells deform near the poles and coastlines follow the resolution of the supplied land mask. Land cells also use continuous shading instead of depth buckets. The exact raw TSV is included unchanged.

The globe is now an oversized, fixed decorative background. The ocean and surrounding canvas are fully transparent, and the land and sample layers have low opacity so the search content stays readable. There are no captions, legends, drag, wheel/pinch zoom, or rotation controls. It ignores pointer input and respects reduced-motion preferences.
The automatic globe motion selects a random starting orientation, spin direction, and speed once per page load. Tilt and roll then stay fixed, with constant yaw rotation and no axis drift or pitch bounce.
